## Core Concepts & Frameworks
- **ADDIE** - Instructional-design process model: Analyze, Design, Develop, Implement, Evaluate. Origin: Florida State University (1975), popularized by the US Armed Forces and ATD.
- **SAM (Successive Approximation Model)** - Iterative agile alternative to ADDIE. Origin: Michael Allen, "Leaving ADDIE for SAM" (Allen Interactions / ASTD Press, 2012).
- **Kirkpatrick Four-Level Training Evaluation Model** - Levels: Reaction, Learning, Behavior, Results. Origin: Donald Kirkpatrick (1959); maintained by Kirkpatrick Partners.
- **Phillips ROI Methodology** - Adds Level 5 (ROI) and isolation of effects to Kirkpatrick. Origin: Jack J. Phillips, ROI Institute.
- **70-20-10 Model** - ~70% experiential / ~20% social / ~10% formal learning. Origin: Lombardo & Eichinger, Center for Creative Leadership (1996).
- **Bloom's Taxonomy** - Cognitive learning-objective levels: Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create (revised by Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001). Origin: Benjamin Bloom (1956).
- **Competency Framework / Dreyfus skill-acquisition** - Defined competencies per proficiency level. Used by SHRM and Dreyfus & Dreyfus (1986).
- **9-Box Grid** - Performance x Potential talent-review matrix. Origin: McKinsey / GE talent management practice.
- **Structured Rubric / Structured-Interview research** - Standardized, evidence-based criteria to reduce subjectivity and bias in promotion decisions.
